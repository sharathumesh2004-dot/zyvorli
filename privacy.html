Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Version 0.9

Plain-language summary. Zyvorli stores only what it needs to run a messenger. Your text messages are end-to-end encrypted when both people have encryption keys. Photos, files and voice messages are private but not yet end-to-end encrypted. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.
Draft notice for the owner. This text describes how version 0.9 actually works. Have a lawyer review it, and fill in the contact details in config.js, before public launch.

1. Who we are

Zyvorli ("we") is a messaging app operated by the business named in the contact section below. You must be 18 or older to use Zyvorli.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising in chats.
  • We do not read your end-to-end encrypted text messages.
  • We do not upload your phone contacts. Matching sends only partial numbers and keeps nothing.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, live delivery and file storage.
  • Brevo: sends the sign-in code email.
  • GitHub Pages: delivers the app files.
  • Google STUN servers (and an optional relay service): help two phones find each other for calls. During a call the other person's phone can see your network address unless a relay is used.

These providers may store data outside India. The storage region is chosen when the project is created.

5. Your control and rights

  • Delete messages: delete for yourself, or for everyone (this also removes photos and files from our storage). Edit a text message within 15 minutes.
  • Download your data: Settings → Your data and privacy → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete my account. This removes your profile, messages you sent and received, calls, files and keys from our systems.
  • Who can find you: default is "My contacts". You can choose Everyone or Nobody.
  • Block and report any person from the chat menu.
  • Security: app lock, two-step verification and a list of logged-in devices with remote sign-out.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your data, and to nominate someone to act for you. Use the contact details below.

6. How long we keep data

Messages and files are kept until you or the other person delete them or the account is deleted. Call signalling data is temporary. Reports are kept as long as needed to deal with abuse and legal duties. Deleted accounts are removed from active systems immediately; backups kept by our providers expire on their own schedule.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Access to every table is restricted by database rules so a person can only read their own chats, calls and files. We limit searches, messages and calls to reduce spam and harvesting of numbers. No system is perfectly secure; please use app lock and keep your devices updated.

8. Legal requests

We may disclose data when the law requires it, and will tell you unless the law forbids it. Because text is end-to-end encrypted, we cannot hand over readable content of those messages.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
